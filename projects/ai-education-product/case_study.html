Example issue

Cannot find a feature

What to check

Is the entry point hard to find, or is the first step missing from the guide? One comment alone does not prove the journey is too long.

Check the situation before drawing a conclusion

My contribution

Add entry-point and step-by-step guidance, group similar questions and identify where clearer explanations are needed.

For product review

Example issue

Unsure how to describe a task

What to check

Is the task clear? Are input requirements explained? Review the actual interaction to find the point of confusion.

Check the situation before drawing a conclusion

My contribution

03
Example issue

Results do not match expectations

What to check

This could relate to inputs, the task or product capability. Understand the actual task and output before attributing it to user misunderstanding.

Check the situation before drawing a conclusion

My contribution

Document the task, steps and specific differences. Clarify existing feature guidance and raise unresolved questions with the team.
