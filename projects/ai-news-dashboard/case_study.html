01 / PROJECT BRIEF

Project context &
my contribution

YouNews brings together news aggregation, topic tracking, keyword monitoring and AI summaries. I used feedback and pricing surveys to understand user needs, planned content for different stages of use, and supported acquisition through campaigns, distribution and creators. Registration and first-topic creation helped inform the next round of content.

User questions, summarised from my approach

Too much information. What is worth following?

Reasoning
Start with scattered information and repetitive searches so people can see whether the product is relevant to them.
Content approach
Use the information-overload campaign to introduce news tracking, then explain the features.
Next step
Explore the product and try it.
